Privacy notice

DuesTrack is the records system {{ org_name }} uses for attendance, fines, weekly dues and payments. Your personal information is processed in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173, "RA 10173"), its implementing rules, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

What we keep

  • Your student number, name, year level, section or group, whether you are an officer, and your enrollment status, taken from the class roster.
  • Your attendance marks at organization events (IN and OUT, from QR scans or officers' entries) and any uniform violations.
  • Fines, waivers, dues, payments and refunds, including GCash reference numbers. No screenshots or photos are collected.
  • For your login: a securely hashed password (never readable), and a log of logins and changes with the address used.

Why

Only to take attendance, compute fines and dues under the organization's rules, record and receipt payments, and give you and the officers an accurate account. The information is not used for anything else, not sold, and not shared outside the organization unless the law requires it.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own record, statement and receipts after logging in. You cannot see anyone else's.
  • Officers see the roster and attendance so they can take attendance. They cannot see payments.
  • The treasurer sees and records fines, waivers, dues and payments.
  • Administrators set the system up.

Apart from the login page and this notice, every page requires a login, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and every change to attendance, money and settings is written to an audit log that cannot be edited.

How long

Records are kept as the organization's financial records after the term ends and are not deleted automatically, so past balances and receipts can always be checked. Daily backups are encrypted.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information kept about you, have mistakes corrected, object to processing, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights or ask a question, contact {{ privacy_contact }}.
